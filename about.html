ABOUT NXT

Where South African gaming
and content creation move forward.

NXT is an independent gaming community and creator platform based in South Africa. We connect players, organisers and creators under one brand.

Our focus

Competition, creator growth and community — without pretending to be a multinational. We build practical tools for real SA organisers and players.

Multi-game

Not locked to a single title. FC, R6, Fortnite, Rocket League, Valorant and more can live on the same platform.

Creator-led

Approved creators can host competitions for their own communities with admin oversight.

Work with us

Partnerships, sponsorships and creator collaborations.
