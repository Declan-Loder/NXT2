Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

NXT ("we") operates a South African gaming and creator platform. We collect account details (email, display name, optional gaming IDs), tournament registrations, payment references and proof uploads, and messages you send via forms.

Data is stored in Supabase and processed on Vercel. Payment card details are handled by the payment provider — we do not store card numbers.

We use your information to run competitions, verify payments, communicate about events and improve the platform. We do not sell personal data.

You may request access or deletion of your account data by contacting NXT through the contact page or Discord, subject to legal retention needs (e.g. payment records).

This policy may be updated as the platform grows. Continued use after changes means you accept the revised policy.
