Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Terms Refunds Shipping Privacy

NXT ("we") operates a South African gaming and creator platform. We collect account details (email, display name, optional gaming IDs), tournament registrations, payment references and proof uploads, merchandise order and delivery details, and messages you send via forms.

Data is stored in Supabase and processed on Vercel. Payment card details are handled by the payment provider — we do not store card numbers.

We use your information to run competitions, process merchandise orders, verify payments, communicate about events and orders, and improve the platform. We do not sell personal data.

You may request access or deletion of your account data by contacting NXT through the contact page, Discord, or at info.nxtgg@gmail.com, subject to legal retention needs (e.g. payment and order records).

This policy may be updated as the platform grows. Continued use after changes means you accept the revised policy.
