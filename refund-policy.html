Refund Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026 · Last updated: 27 November 2026

2.1 Our commitment

At NXT, we want customers to be satisfied with their purchases. Because NXT merchandise is produced on a made-to-order basis, each order is submitted for production after payment has been confirmed.

This policy explains how cancellations, returns, exchanges and refunds are handled. Nothing in this policy removes or limits any rights customers have under applicable South African consumer-protection laws.

2.2 Order cancellations

If you wish to cancel an order, please contact NXT as soon as possible at info.nxtgg@gmail.com.

If the order has not yet been submitted to the production supplier, NXT will assess whether the cancellation can be processed. Once production has started, cancellation may no longer be possible in circumstances where the product is being made to the customer's specifications or is clearly personalised, subject to applicable law.

This does not affect any statutory cancellation or refund rights that may apply.

2.3 Incorrect, damaged or defective products

If your order arrives damaged, defective or different from what you ordered, please contact us. When reporting an issue, please provide:

  • Your order number.
  • A description of the problem.
  • Clear photographs where the issue can be shown visually.
  • Your contact details.

NXT will review the issue and work with the customer to arrange an appropriate solution. Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, this may include a replacement product, a repair where appropriate, a refund, or another remedy agreed with the customer.

Customers are not required to accept a replacement where applicable consumer law entitles them to another remedy. Under the Consumer Protection Act, qualifying defective goods may be returned within six months of delivery, subject to the applicable statutory requirements. The appropriate remedy depends on the circumstances.

2.4 Change-of-mind returns

For qualifying online purchases, the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act provides a cooling-off right that generally allows a customer to cancel within seven days after receiving the goods, without giving a reason.

There are statutory exceptions, including certain goods made to the consumer's specifications or clearly personalised. Where this cooling-off right applies, the customer may be responsible for the direct cost of returning the goods, as provided for by law.

This right is separate from the customer's rights regarding defective or incorrectly supplied goods.

2.5 Exchanges

If you selected the wrong size or product option, contact NXT at info.nxtgg@gmail.com.

Exchanges for change-of-mind or sizing reasons will be assessed based on the circumstances, product condition and applicable legal rights. Where an exchange is not legally required and is not approved by NXT, the customer may need to place a new order. Incorrect or defective products will be handled in accordance with the customer's applicable legal rights.

2.6 Refund processing

Where a refund is approved or legally required, NXT will confirm the refund arrangement with the customer. Refunds will generally be processed using the original payment method where possible.

Where a refund is required under the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act's cooling-off provisions, the applicable statutory refund period is 30 days from cancellation. Other refunds will be processed within the timeframe required by applicable law or communicated to the customer when the refund is arranged. Bank processing times may vary.

2.7 Unavailable products

If NXT cannot fulfil an order because a product is unavailable or the supplier cannot produce it, the customer will be notified. NXT will arrange an appropriate solution, including a refund where required by law.

2.8 How to request a return or refund

Please contact:

Email: info.nxtgg@gmail.com
Subject: NXT Return / Refund Request

Please include your order number, the reason for your request and any relevant supporting photographs. NXT will review the request and respond with the next steps.
