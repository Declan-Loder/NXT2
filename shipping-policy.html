Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026 · Last updated: 27 November 2026
Contact: info.nxtgg@gmail.com

Terms Refunds Shipping Privacy

3.1 Overview

NXT currently operates its merchandise store on a made-to-order basis. This means products are submitted to our production supplier after an order has been placed and payment has been confirmed. Our goal is to provide customers with clear information about production, dispatch and delivery.

3.2 Production timeframe

The estimated production turnaround time provided by our supplier is 5–7 working days. This period begins after payment and the necessary order details have been confirmed and the order has been submitted for production.

Please note:

  • Production time is not the same as delivery time.
  • Weekends and public holidays are not included in working days.
  • Production may take longer during busy periods or where supplier delays occur.
  • Customers will be informed if NXT becomes aware of a significant delay.

3.3 Delivery methods

NXT may use available third-party delivery services, which may include Pudo, The Courier Guy, and other suitable courier services where available.

The delivery method will depend on the customer's location, the size of the order and the available courier options. The available delivery options and applicable charges will be communicated before the order is finalised.

3.4 Delivery costs

Delivery charges are separate from product prices unless expressly stated otherwise. The applicable delivery fee will be shown or communicated to the customer before payment is completed.

Delivery fees may vary depending on delivery location, parcel size and weight, courier service selected, and collection or delivery method. NXT will not knowingly add undisclosed delivery charges after an order has been confirmed.

3.5 Delivery timeframe

Once production is complete, the order will be prepared for dispatch using the selected delivery arrangement. The delivery timeframe depends on the courier service, destination and operational conditions.

The supplier's estimated production period of 5–7 working days does not include courier transit time. Any delivery estimate provided by NXT or a courier is an estimate and not a guaranteed delivery date, unless expressly confirmed otherwise.

3.6 Delivery information

Customers must provide accurate delivery and contact information when placing an order. NXT cannot be held responsible for delays caused by incorrect or incomplete information supplied by the customer, subject to applicable law.

If a delivery cannot be completed because the information provided is incorrect or the recipient is unavailable, additional delivery arrangements or charges may be necessary.

3.7 Tracking and order updates

Where tracking information is provided by the courier, NXT will share the available tracking details with the customer. Customers may contact NXT if they need assistance with an order or delivery update.

Email: info.nxtgg@gmail.com

3.8 Delayed or missing deliveries

If your order has not arrived within the expected timeframe, please contact NXT. We will review the order status and, where applicable, follow up with the supplier or courier.

If an order cannot be delivered or fulfilled within the agreed or legally applicable timeframe, the customer will be entitled to the remedies available under South African law.

3.9 Collection and courier arrangements

Where a collection-based delivery option is selected, customers are responsible for following the courier's collection instructions and collection deadlines. Any additional charges resulting from missed collection deadlines or incorrect collection details will be assessed based on the courier's applicable terms and the circumstances.
